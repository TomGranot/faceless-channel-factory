# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers should make participation safe, respectful, and useful. Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- protecting private account, customer, and provider information;
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and project events. It also applies when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock threads, issue warnings, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. They should explain the reason when doing so does not expose private information or create further harm.

Report a conduct concern privately through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting workflow. Prefix the summary with `CONDUCT:` and include links or screenshots needed to review the event. The maintainers will limit disclosure to the people required to investigate and respond.

Good-faith reports will not trigger retaliation. Knowingly false or abusive reports may themselves violate this standard.
